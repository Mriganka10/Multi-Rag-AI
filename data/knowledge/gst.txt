Section 73 of the GST law generally deals with determination of tax not paid, short paid, erroneously refunded, or input tax credit wrongly availed or utilized for reasons other than fraud or wilful misstatement.

Section 74 generally applies where fraud, wilful misstatement, or suppression of facts is alleged.

A professional SCN reply should identify the allegations, reconcile facts with records, address limitation and jurisdiction where relevant, and request personal hearing if adverse action is proposed.
